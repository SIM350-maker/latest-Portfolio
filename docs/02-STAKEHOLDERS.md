## Primary Stakeholders
- **Recruiter**: Goals include identifying qualified candidates efficiently, verifying skill claims, and presenting suitable candidates to hiring managers. Needs: clear professional identity, evidence of relevant skills, easy access to CV/resume, contact information.
- **Hiring Manager**: Goals include assessing technical fit, engineering judgment, and team compatibility. Needs: evidence of project impact, understanding of architectural decisions, ability to verify claims, insight into problem-solving approach.
- **Technical Interviewer**: Goals include evaluating technical depth, problem-solving skills, and engineering rigor. Needs: detailed project evidence, traceable claims, architecture and design documentation, testing and deployment evidence.
- **Potential Client**: Goals include assessing ability to solve specific problems, verifying relevant experience, and establishing trust. Needs: relevant project examples, evidence of production concerns understanding, clear communication of capabilities, contact information.
- **Engineer / Portfolio Owner**: Goals include showcasing capabilities, maintaining professional evidence over time, updating portfolio efficiently, and controlling narrative. Needs: ability to add/update evidence, simple publishing process, verifiable presentation of work, long-term maintainability.

## Secondary Stakeholders
- **Collaborator**: Wants to understand the engineer's role and contributions in past projects.
- **Mentor**: Interested in the engineer's growth and learning trajectory.
- **Professional Network**: May seek to endorse or verify skills.
- **Search Engine**: Needs to index content for discoverability.
- **Future Employer**: Will evaluate the engineer's historical work and growth.
- **Future Self (Maintaining System)**: Needs a system that remains maintainable and understandable over years.
