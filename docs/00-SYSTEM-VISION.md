## Vision Statement
To create a portfolio system that makes a software engineer's capabilities observable, understandable, credible, and verifiable to professional audiences through evidence-based presentation.

## Core Objectives
- Provide verifiable evidence of engineering skills and experience.
- Enable different stakeholders to quickly assess the engineer's suitability for their needs.
- Support the engineer in maintaining and updating their professional evidence over time.
- Ensure the portfolio itself demonstrates engineering quality and rigor.

## Success Criteria
- A recruiter can understand the engineer's professional identity and key strengths within 30 seconds.
- A technical evaluator can trace claims to evidence for at least one project.
- The engineer can update the portfolio with new evidence without modifying application source code (if determined to be a requirement).
- The deployed portfolio meets accessibility and performance standards.
